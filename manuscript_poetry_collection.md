## Manuscript

*[image]*

For you, I will destroy the universe  
also for you, I can restore it  
For you, I will keep myself alive  
to hold you till the end  
For you, I will burn every flower  
also for you, I can make it bloom again  

For you, I wield the power to erase,  
Unraveling galaxies in an infinite chase.  
Yet, in your name, I unfold cosmic threads,  
Restoring the cosmos from where darkness spreads.  

For you, I'm a guardian, a cosmic guide,  
Preserving existence with hope as my side.  
I'll endure through eons, a timeless vow,  
For you, I stay, from then until now.  

For you, I'd wield a destructive might,  
A violent being, shadows in flight.  
To Kill the World, in a melancholic tune,  
Yet for you, I birth a blossom in June.  

For you, I'll dance the stars in cosmic ballet,  
A celestial symphony, night and day.  
In your name, the universe I'll adorn,  
A kaleidoscope of beauty, reborn.  

For you, the cosmos bends and swings,,  
A testament to love that forever stays.  
In the tapestry of time, our story weaves,  
For you, the universe both dies and breathes  

And when the flames have all been spent and gone,  
From ashes rise, a new and vibrant dawn.  
For you, I'll make it bloom again, I swear.

## Divine Letter

You are the universe in ecstatic motion,  
a manuscript of a divine letter.  
You are the cosmic dance, the rhythm of stars,  
a choreography written in the language of charms.  

You are the verses of a celestial poem,  
a shimmering constellation in the night.  
You are the touch of stardust,  
a painting across the canvas.  

You are the heartbeat of existence,  
an echo of love wavering in the distance.  
You are the symphony on a moonlit night,  
a harmonious note playing in the cosmic delight.  

You are the story scripted in the chaos,  
a whisper exceeding the shadows.  
In every breath, every heartbeat, and every dawn,  
you are the melody, the universe's eternal song

## From River to Winter

*[image]*

Empty park swing, craking in the breeze  
A melody of misery setting my spirit freeze.  
For in a winter mind, a curious truth I find,  
The bravest hands are those, that welcome the cold  

For courage isn't just a single wink,  
It's facing shadows, facing what's incomplete.  
The strength to turn the page, though every word stings true,  
Knowing some stories flicker bright, then fade to endless blue.  

The truth, a viper coiled, its venom sweet,  
You craved its kiss, though knowing it would cheat.  
Each breath a waste, a loser in time's fold,  
Hours crawl by, a story yet untold.  

The truth, a sharp that pierced a wounded dream,  
You craved its light, though cutting what did gleam.  
Each breath a sigh, a prisoner of what's past,  
The clock ticks heavy, moments built to last.  

The path is blurry, veiled in mist,  
But hope, a fragile ember, still insists.  
The river flows, a promise to the sea,  
Carries the weight, the burden used to be.  

Let tears be acid, eat away the past,  
Leave scars that whisper, memories that last.  
This river's current, a relentless tide,  
Will drag you under, where secrets reside.  

These purifying waves cleanse away the day's,  
The burden carried through the longest night.  
Let them fall like whispers on a mournful breeze,  
Let be embers, fuming in the night,  
A fuel of longing, burning ever bright.  

This river's current, a relentless flow,  
Carries unspoken whispers, seeds that won't grow.  
Will strength emerge from the vulnerability's core?  
Can shattered pieces mend and rise once more?  
Perhaps the answer lies beyond the winter.

## Hollow Shell

Cracked reflection in the glass,  
Empty eyes, a hollow shell,  
Haunted by the shadows cast,  
Of a love that couldn't dwell.  

Whispers echo in the night,  
Laughter lost, a fading dream,  
Each ragged breath, a fading light,  
Drowning in a silent scream.  

Coffee cup dreams, still warm in my grip,  
Your name on the mug, like a ship on the slip.  
Always held tight but never dared to take flight,  
The melody buzzed, just out of sight.  

A single Sigh, a salty sting,  
Scars that etch a painful lore,  
A gentle release, a whisper unknown,  
The sky fades soft, leaving me on my own.

Among the chaos, find the peace.  

Among the shadows, find the light,  
Like the sunrays, through the night.

Silent echoes, whispers of the beat,

## Dissection

Is bravery a scalpel, sharp and cold,  
Dissecting hope, leaving stories old?  
Or is it letting go, a cruel delight,  
To drown in what once burned so bright?  

Is bravery a whisper, soft and old,  
Admitting futures fractured, growing old?  
Or is it embracing the chaos, a bitter ache,  
To watch you paint a world I couldn't take?  

Each breath a countdown, every hour a waiting bay.  
Is bravery letting go, or holding on in pain?

## Lost Answer: Ghost of Warmth

For the ghost of warmth in a cooling glass,  
I raise the wine to memories that pass.  
For the ache of absence in a crowded room,  
I search for ease in the fading bloom.  

For the sting of tears beneath a starless sky,  
I trace the lines where constellations lie.  
Each glittering drop, a diamond in the night,  
Reflecting echoes of the faded light.  

Did walls rise high, or bridges fall away?  
The answer is lost, still today

## Lost Answer: Truth known

But in the ashes, stories start to rise,  
Of lessons learned beneath the moonless skies.  
For it's true weight isn't what we hold,  
But what we cherish, even when it's old.  

For the hollow echo of a whispered name,  
I walk through the ashes of blame.  
For in the letting go, a truth is known,  
The heart crumbles for what the soul has sown.  

So let me raise to skies, though tears may fall,  
To a love that burned, though destined to null.

## Painted Sky

The sun bleeds out, a crimson tear,  
Across the canvas of the day,  
It paints red, for a year,  
In hues of passion, I went astray.  

Trauma etched its claws on souls,  
Shattering dreams, once held so near,  
The red I knew, turned into coals,  
Leaving scars, and ashes, and fear.  

But in the embers, doubts arises,  
A single spark, defying fate,  
The red sky fades, the morning writes,  
A different chapter, on a clean slate.  

For even after sunsets fade,  
The skies go dark, and rain starts pouring,  
Though memories echo, bittersweet,  
The moon paints paths that meet.

## Home

*[image]*

Footprints in the frosted sand,  
Whispering secrets I can't withstand.  
A hollow ring where laughter danced,  
A melody of memories now entranced.  

Rain on a window pane,  
Blurring visions, easing pain.  
I held too tight, now shadows roam,  
But in letting go, she finds her own home.  

In dreams you linger,  
A haunting echo in this lonely space.  
Perhaps the truest cruelty is letting you be,  
A masterpiece I could never fully see.  

So let me breathe, and let the river flow,  
Release the hold, and watch the sky grow.  
For in this torment, a pleasure I confess,  
To watch you crumble, utterly possessed.

## Within the World I Define

In the shadows of the night,  
It burns with a wicked light,  
A twisted flame that burns bright,  
though the truth runs dry.  

It clings to thoughts I can't let go,  
A tether bound to all I know.  
It beat out a haunting tune,  
A melody that's out of tune,  
A relentless chaos I cannot stop,  
That's doomed to end in loss.  

In every sleep, a heavy trace,  
That's born of dark disgrace,  
A quiet pull, a breathless tide,  
A force too bold to run or hide.  

A quiet voice inside my veins.  
A perfect script I've built,  
A world that only I define,  
A story only I construe.  

Let it blaze, untamed, unseen,  
To light the spaces in between.  
For even if it fades by dawn,  
Its fleeting warmth is never gone.  

So let me guard what feels so true,  
And keep the world from reaching you.

## Controlled Ruin

The sea burned long before we spoke-  
still, black, unmoving,  
like something that forgot how to choke.  

Between us stretches that charred sea,  
Too still to drown, too dead to be.  
You say that ash can learn to bloom,  
That life still breathes in broken room.  

I watch it crack, the surface thin,  
A quiet fracture folding in.  
"You don't need to destroy it all,"  
You speak like something might still call.  

But I don't burn what fully dies,  
Just half-formed truths and almost-lies.  
You kneel where nothing shows your face,  
I stand where smoke won't try to trace.  

"Look closer," you insist, like plea,  
"There's something left, there has to be."  
There is. There always is. That seed,  
That stubborn, unforgiving need.  

You name it hope. I leave it bare.  
No gentle word will take it there.  
So I scorch the edges, slow, exact,  
Watch it shrink, but still react.  

And in that twitch, that faint defy,  
I see the part that will not die.  
"You're scared," you say.  
No. I'm aware.  
Fear hesitates. I'm never there.  

The charred sea shifts, not wave, not tide,  
Just truth we never let collide.  
You step too close, you always do,  
As if there's something left of you.  

"If this goes on, there's nothing left"  
Your voice, almost of something kept.  
No "us" to lose, no past to save,  
Just you who builds,  
and me who gave  
it's all to flame.

## Out of frame

It didn't start as anything important,  
just a habit of noticing more than needed,  
reading what wasn't offered,  
fixing what was never asked.  

I stayed close without arriving  
close enough to map your days,  
far enough to never exist inside them,  
a presence that learned how to be useful  
without ever being chosen.  

Somewhere in that, I started admiring the distance itself,  
the way you lived in a world I could observe but not enter,  
and I convinced myself that staying outside  
was still a kind of staying.  

Time didn't move forward so much as repeat,  
like a thought I kept rethinking until it lost its origin,  
until I couldn't tell whether I was remembering you  
or reconstructing the idea of having been near you.  

And when I tried to separate truth from interpretation,  
there was less left than I expected.  

I think that's when I understood  
I was never part of what I was trying to be,  
only part of what it looked like from outside.  

And even then, I stayed.  
because leaving would have required  
admitting I had been standing in a place  
that never had space for me in the first place.  

So I kept observing.  
Kept refining.  
Kept turning everything into something I could carry  
without breaking it or myself too visibly.  

And what remained wasn't clarity or chaos,  
not even distance anymore  
just a mind that had learned too well  
how to understand everything it could never belong to.

## Silence, in layers

I tried to hold a single thought,  
But it split before it ever caught,  
Not loud, not sharp, just slightly wrong  
A shift I couldn't hold for long.  

A word would start, then lose its place,  
A meaning slip without a trace,  
As if my mind forgot the rule  
Of staying still, of being whole.  

Even silence wasn't clean  
It came in layers I had seen  
But never meant to notice through,  
Each one pretending to be true.  

I'd look at something simple, plain,  
And watch it fracture in my brain,  
Not breaking outside, not in view  
Just something only I could do.  

And worse than noise was when it paused,  
That empty space with no clear cause,  
Where nothing moved, yet something spun  
Behind the sense of being done.  

Even time began to blur  
Not fast, not slow, just hard to cure,  
Like every moment overlapped  
And nothing ever stayed intact.  

I tried to focus, tried to stay,  
But thought kept folding in the way  
A loop repeats until it learns  
To turn itself into its turns.  

And somewhere in that quiet strain,  
I stopped distinguishing the pain  
From all the things I over-read  
Until they both felt in my head.  

Not broken. Not exactly lost.  
Just everything at once, and crossed.

## Things I Learn to Bury

They teach us usefulness.  
Endurance.  
How to survive embarrassment  
without visibly bleeeding from it.  

You learn eaarly  
that grief is acceptable only in controlled quantities.  
That tenderness must arrive disguised as humor,  
or loyalty, or silence.  
Especially silence.  

So I became excellent at silence.  
Excellent at smiling normally,  
while entire emotional catastrophes unfolded beneath the surface.  
Excellent at saying "it's fine"  
with the precisio n of practiced self-erasure.  

And sometimes I wonder  
how many men have mistaken emotional suppression for maturity  
simply because nobody ever handed them a safer language for pain.  

There were nights  
I wanted someone to hold my face gently  
and say:  
you do not always have to survive things alone.  

But the desire itself felt humiliating.  
Like begging for something small enough  
that you should have outgrown needing it years ago.  

And maybe that is the tragedy.  
Not that I do not feel deeply.  
That feel deeply in complete isolation.  
Like oceans trapped beneath ice,  
visible only through fractures.

## Rage in cage

There is a particular kind of rage  
that never becomes loud.  
It calcifies instead.  
Settles into the body  
like ash inside the lungs.  

Not explosive. Just constant.  

I carried anger for years  
quietly,  
obediently,  
allowing it to alter the architecture of my inner life.  

Anger at being misunderstood.  
At being emotionally necessary only when convenient.  
At always translating myself carefully enough  
to remain digestible.  

Sometimes I wanted to scream.  
honestly enough  
to finally rupture the performance.  

But rage becomes dangerous  
when you are taught your entire life  
That tenderness is conditional.  

So instead,  
I learned restraint so thoroughly  
it began resembling passivity even to me.  

And that is how some people disappear emotionally,  
through excessive containment.  
Like stars collapsing inward from their own gravity.

## Between Midnight & Sleep

No-  
You happen to me  
in every dark-haired silhouette beneath streetlights  
in every careful voice soft enough to resemble mercy  
in the unbearable architecture of ordinary things  
in cups left warm beside unfinished conversations  
in the pause between midnight & sleep  
in every of your seen-zone, your ignoring  
in my inner voice repeating the phase  

You happened to me  
in the eye of you meeting mine below the clock  
in those passing glances that linger half a second too long  
in the soft distortion of memory near midnight  

You happen to me,  
with the tired eyes of people in afternoon Campus,  
with the spaces between conversations,  
with the songs that have nothing to do with you.  

You happen to me in a world that doesn't contain u  
You happen to be me inside the texture of my ordinary life itself

## The Blue Inside Ruin

I used to associate you with blue.  
Not you as you are now,  
but a version of you that only ever existed  
inside my faith in what you could become.  
A cleaner version. A softer version.  
A version untouched by the things  
Not the one that killed the kind in me  
that slowly darkened your hands and heart.  

Its the blue of deep oceans where light enters softly  
The glazing blue of evening skies  
right before the world becomes quiet enough  
for lonely thoughts to grow louder.  
The blue from standing at the edge of sea at night.  
Something visible yet unreachable,  
The blue of winter skies  
just before snow or rain begins to fall.  
The blue of glaciers  
hidden beneath layers of ancient ice,  
The blue of moonlight on rivers,  
breaking itself apart across moving water  
The blue of stained glass in cathedrals,  
when afternoon sunlight passes through it.  
The blue of Earth seen from space,  
fragile and luminous against endless blackness.  

Maybe you carried too much ruin to ever become the charred sea. Maybe I loved a  
version of you untouched by the things you eventually chose.  
Maybe I only painted you that way  
because I needed something beautiful enough  
to devote myself to completely.  

And maybe I was never worthy of the charred sea either.  
Not of holding it. Not of reaching it.  
Only of standing far away from it  
like someone staring at the sea at night,  
wanting desperately to deserve its stillness.  

The blue of charred sea.
