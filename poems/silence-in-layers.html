Silence, in layers

even silence wasn't clean

I tried to hold a single thought,
But it split before it ever caught,
Not loud, not sharp, just slightly wrong
A shift I couldn't hold for long.

A word would start, then lose its place,
A meaning slip without a trace,
As if my mind forgot the rule
Of staying still, of being whole.

Even silence wasn't clean
It came in layers I had seen
But never meant to notice through,
Each one pretending to be true.

I'd look at something simple, plain,
And watch it fracture in my brain,
Not breaking outside, not in view
Just something only I could do.

And worse than noise was when it paused,
That empty space with no clear cause,
Where nothing moved, yet something spun
Behind the sense of being done.

Even time began to blur
Not fast, not slow, just hard to cure,
Like every moment overlapped
And nothing ever stayed intact.

I tried to focus, tried to stay,
But thought kept folding in the way
A loop repeats until it learns
To turn itself into its turns.

And somewhere in that quiet strain,
I stopped distinguishing the pain
From all the things I over-read
Until they both felt in my head.

Not broken. Not exactly lost.
Just everything at once, and crossed.

tai khan
