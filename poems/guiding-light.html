Guiding Light

hope, like stars, guiding each day

Amidst the sunrise is gentle bliss,
Stars fade, but ours remains endless.
Mornings bring hope, a chance to release,
Among the chaos, find the peace.

Among the shadows, find the light,
Like the sunrays, through the night.
Hope like stars, guiding each day,
Underneath the cloud, which convey.

Silent echoes, whispers of the beat,
Nourished in passion, ignore the retreat.
And find the skies, enjoy the views.

tai khan
