Landscape

soar on wings, reach the skies

Mornings bring hope, a chance to release
Embrace the chaos, find the ease.
Make a difference, let passion grow.
Inspect the seven letters, let the letters flow

(seven letters sealed in May)

Beneath the rain, let freedom rise.
Soar on wings, reach the skies.
A renewed might, with every sunrise,
And find the landscape, enjoy the views.
