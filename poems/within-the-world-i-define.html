Within the World I Define

a twisted flame that burns bright

In the shadows of the night,
It burns with a wicked light,
A twisted flame that burns bright,
though the truth runs dry.

It clings to thoughts I can't let go,
A tether bound to all I know.
It beat out a haunting tune,
A melody that's out of tune,
A relentless chaos I cannot stop,
That's doomed to end in loss.

In every sleep, a heavy trace,
That's born of dark disgrace,
A quiet pull, a breathless tide,
A force too bold to run or hide.

A quiet voice inside my veins.
A perfect script I've built,
A world that only I define,
A story only I construe.

Let it blaze, untamed, unseen,
To light the spaces in between.
For even if it fades by dawn,
Its fleeting warmth is never gone.

So let me guard what feels so true,
And keep the world from reaching you.
