Every Winter

cold hands, silent rooms

Empty hand, cold and still,
Like fire gone, memories chill.
Sun hid away, winter's hold,
Stories I knew, turned story told.

Silent room, no laughter near,
Only echoes, dreams disappear.
Leaves that fall, dance on the breeze,
Winters may gone, for a chance to breathe.
