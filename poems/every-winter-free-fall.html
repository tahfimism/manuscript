Every Winter: Free Fall

diamond drops, without a scheme

Moonless night, tears freely fall,
Stars above, seem distant all.
Diamond drops, without a scheme,
Fading light, a broken dream.

Ashes rise, stories unfold,
But it remains, in heart and soul.
So raise the drink, for what we dreamed,
Lessons learned, for everything told.

tai khan
