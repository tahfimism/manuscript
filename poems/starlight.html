№ 08  ·  Starlight

Starlight

a bridge between night and morning

Moonlit nights, with
A symphony of stars,
Seeking a heart
that beats in the dark.

So let the symphony play,
And guide us to the day,
Where hearts may meet,
Beneath the moonlit, souls entwine.

tai khan
