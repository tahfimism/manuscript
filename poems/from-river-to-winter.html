From River to Winter

a long current, freezing at the edges

Empty park swing, craking in the breeze
A melody of misery setting my spirit freeze.
For in a winter mind, a curious truth I find,
The bravest hands are those, that welcome the cold

For courage isn't just a single wink,
It's facing shadows, facing what's incomplete.
The strength to turn the page, though every word stings true,
Knowing some stories flicker bright, then fade to endless blue.

The truth, a viper coiled, its venom sweet,
You craved its kiss, though knowing it would cheat.
Each breath a waste, a loser in time's fold,
Hours crawl by, a story yet untold.

The truth, a sharp that pierced a wounded dream,
You craved its light, though cutting what did gleam.
Each breath a sigh, a prisoner of what's past,
The clock ticks heavy, moments built to last.

The path is blurry, veiled in mist,
But hope, a fragile ember, still insists.
The river flows, a promise to the sea,
Carries the weight, the burden used to be.

Let tears be acid, eat away the past,
Leave scars that whisper, memories that last.
This river's current, a relentless tide,
Will drag you under, where secrets reside.

These purifying waves cleanse away the day's,
The burden carried through the longest night.
Let them fall like whispers on a mournful breeze,
Let be embers, fuming in the night,
A fuel of longing, burning ever bright.

This river's current, a relentless flow,
Carries unspoken whispers, seeds that won't grow.
Will strength emerge from the vulnerability's core?
Can shattered pieces mend and rise once more?
Perhaps the answer lies beyond the winter.

tai khan
